# MGQ Paradox Mod Collection

Small mods for Monster Girl Quest! Paradox RPG, one folder each. They are Patch folder mods ("Type 1"), so each needs the community's mod loader: the `Patch.rb` from [*Patch.rb (enable Type 1 mods)*](https://mgq.miraheze.org/wiki/Paradox_mods#Patch.rb_(enable_Type_1_mods)) on the MGQ wiki.

| Mod | What it does | Download |
|---|---|---|
| [Battle Dialogue](Battle_Dialogue) | Shows what is said in battle in boxes at the sides of the screen instead of the message window, so the battle never stops for a key press. Requires the [Mod Framework](https://mgqonline.org/mods/mod-framework). | [Battle_Dialogue.rb](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Paradox-Mod-Collection/releases/latest/download/Battle_Dialogue.rb) |
| [Invasions](Invasions) | Lets more monsters of the area join a random encounter or a boss fight while you fight it, by a chance after each turn. | [Invasions.rb](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Paradox-Mod-Collection/releases/latest/download/Invasions.rb) |
| [Level Cap](Level_Cap) | Caps your level until the next story boss falls, and lets your characters spend points on job and race slots per tier. | [Level_Cap.rb](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Paradox-Mod-Collection/releases/latest/download/Level_Cap.rb) |
| [Map Display](Map_Display) | Shows the name of the map in the top left corner after each map change. Requires the [Mod Framework](https://mgqonline.org/mods/mod-framework). | [Map_Display.rb](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Paradox-Mod-Collection/releases/latest/download/Map_Display.rb) |
| [Now Playing](Now_Playing) | Shows the name of the music in the top right corner whenever a new track starts. | [Now_Playing.rb](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Paradox-Mod-Collection/releases/latest/download/Now_Playing.rb) |
| [Party Sheet](Party_Sheet) | Writes a page with every party member's picture, stats, equipment, abilities and trait at the press of a hotkey, plus an image of the Frontline to share, and can upload it to mgqonline.org for 7 days. | [Party_Sheet.rb](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Paradox-Mod-Collection/releases/latest/download/Party_Sheet.rb) |
| [TimeStop Music](TimeStop_Music) | Keeps the battle music playing through time stop. | [TimeStop_Music.rb](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Paradox-Mod-Collection/releases/latest/download/TimeStop_Music.rb) |

To install one, download its `.rb` file and put it into the game's `Patch` folder. To uninstall it, delete that file.

## Requirements

Map Display and Battle Dialogue require the [Mod Framework](https://mgqonline.org/mods/mod-framework). Invasions, Level Cap, Now Playing and Party Sheet run without it and use it when it is installed: the options of Invasions, Level Cap and Party Sheet are then in the game's options under **Mods**, Party Sheet's hotkey sits among the hotkeys of every mod, Now Playing's box sits below other boxes in the top right corner, and Level Cap adds a **Level Cap** tab to the World overview of [Monster Girl Quest! Online](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Online) 0.7 or later. It comes on its own, not with the Collection or MGQ Online: install it as its page says.

## More mods

- [Mod Framework](https://mgqonline.org/mods/mod-framework): loads your mods and shows their options in the game's options under **Mods**.
- [Discord Rich Presence](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Paradox-Discord-Rich-Presence): shows what you're doing in the game on your Discord profile.
- [Monster Girl Quest! Online](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Online): lets you play the game together with friends.
