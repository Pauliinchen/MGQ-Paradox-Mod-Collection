# Level Cap

Caps your characters' base level until the next story boss falls, and lets each character spend points on job and race slots. Beating a boss on the list raises the cap and the points, so the story's fights stay a challenge without grinding past them, and every build is a plan.

### Level cap

- A new game starts with a cap of 5. Each boss on the list raises it once beaten, and the cap is always the highest one you have reached, so beating a later boss early raises it straight away.
- EXP past the cap is lost, and EXP items have no effect at the cap. Menus show each level with its maximum (**Lv 1 / 5**, the cap for the base level), yellow once reached, and the status screen shows it (**25/25**) where it would show **COMPLETE**.
- No level is ever lowered. Companions who join above the cap, and levels the story sets itself (like the party lifted to 64 at the Great Decision), stay as they are; those characters gain no EXP until the cap passes them.

### Job and race points

- Every character has the points of the highest boss you have beaten (see the table below). They spend them on **slots**: each slot holds one job or race of its tier.

  | Tier | Job slot | Race slot |
  | --- | ---: | ---: |
  | Basic | 1 | 2 |
  | Intermediate | 2 | 4 |
  | Advanced | 4 | 8 |
  | Sealed | 6 | 12 |
  | Forbidden | 12 | 24 |

- One Basic job slot and one Basic race slot are free. A tier only takes slots once the story has opened it: Intermediate jobs at the Four bandits, Intermediate races at the Micaela event, Advanced at Salamander, Sealed at Garuda, Forbidden races at Kagetsumugi and Forbidden jobs at Uriela on the Chaos route.
- In the job change screen, pick a character and choose **Allocate**. ←/→ add or remove a slot of the tier under the cursor. A slot that holds a job or race can't be removed; reset instead.
- A job or race takes a slot once it reaches level 2, and the one a character wears always does. A new one can only be taken while its tier has a free slot; the job change screen shows each tier's slots on its tab (**Basic 2/3**: two taken, three slots), greys out the rest and tells which tier is full.
- **Reset** in the same menu gives back every point. The character keeps one Basic job and one Basic race of your choice; all other jobs and races go, and so does every skill and ability a job or race teaches, Proofs included, unless the kept job or race teaches it. Learn the jobs and races again to get them back. The base level stays. Other Basic races the character had stay available at level 1, since a character can only ever take the Basic races they have had.
- With [Monster Girl Quest! Online](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Online) installed, its World overview gets a **Level Cap** tab: your characters with their free points. **Enter** on a character moves to their slots per tier, where **Left** removes a slot and **Right** adds one (green: room left, yellow: full, red: more classes than slots), and the **Reset** button beside their name resets them, the same as in the job change screen. **Ctrl** goes back to the characters.
- The mod remembers how many jobs and races of each tier a character has ever mastered. Taking up a job or race in such a tier masters it at once, until that count is reached again, so a reset never means grinding the same levels twice. **Allocate** shows the count per tier.
- Jobs and races from the story, like those Luka gets at the Micaela event, take slots too. If a tier then holds more than its slots, it shows in orange and takes nothing new until you add slots or reset.
- The final battle of the Chaos route lifts the level cap and gives 50 more points; the slots still apply.
- Whether a boss is beaten comes from the game's own story switches and variables, the same ones that open its rematch in Hades. Saves from before you installed the mod get the cap and points they have earned.

**When you install it, every character in your party and in the Pocket Castle spends their points once**, and so does each companion when they join: they keep their worn job and race (or a Basic one where the points can't pay for it), then a job and a race by turns, each time the highest tier their points still pay for, until the points run out. A job or race only stays together with the jobs and races it requires, paid for with them, and goes if one of those is missing. The jobs and races the points don't cover go, with the skills and abilities only they taught; everything they had mastered still counts toward instant mastery. A Reset afterwards gives all the points back to spend yourself.

## Options

With [Mod Config Remake](../0_ModConfigRemake) or the Mod Config Menu, **Level Cap** in the mod options turns the cap on or off, and **Job and Race Points** below it the points and slots. Both are on by default and are kept in each save.

## Install

1. Install the community's mod loader: the `Patch.rb` from [*Patch.rb (enable Type 1 mods)*](https://mgq.miraheze.org/wiki/Paradox_mods#Patch.rb_(enable_Type_1_mods)) on the MGQ wiki, in your `Patch` folder. If you already use other Patch folder mods, you have it.
2. [Download `Level_Cap.rb`](https://github.com/Pauliinchen/MGQ-Paradox-Mod-Collection/releases/latest/download/Level_Cap.rb) and put it into the `Patch` folder.

To uninstall, delete `Patch\Level_Cap.rb`. Your levels, jobs and races stay as they are; jobs and races a reset took away don't come back.

## Compatibility

Tested on 3.06 with the English translation.

The mod replaces no game methods. It runs alongside these, leaving what they do unchanged, and hooks them once the first scene starts, so it also wraps the versions the newer translation's plugins define:

- `Game_Actor#max_level`, to hold the base level at the cap
- `Game_Interpreter#level_adjust` and `#command_316`, so levels the story sets go past the cap
- `Foo::JobChange::Window_ClassName#class_change_enable?`, to grey out jobs and races of a full tier
- `Foo::JobChange::Window_Popup#make_command_list` and `#window_height`, for Allocate and Reset
- `Scene_JobChange#start`, `#create_popup_window` and `#process_class_ok`, and `Scene_Map#start`, to reset newly met characters, open the allocation and master a job or race as it is taken up
- `Foo::JobChange::Window_ClassType#draw_item`, `#command_name` and `#update`, for the counts on the tier tabs
- `Foo::JobChange::Window_ClassStatus#draw_status`, and `#draw_page_title` of the translation's paged job details, to tell which tier is full
- `#draw_actor_level` of `Window_Base` and of every window that draws levels its own way, to show each level with its maximum
- `Window_Base#draw_actor_simple_status` and `#draw_text`, to show the cap on the status screen and make room for the maximum
